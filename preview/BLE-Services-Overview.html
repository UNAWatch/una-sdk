UNA Watch — BLE Services Overview

This is the map of the GATT services a UNA Watch exposes over Bluetooth Low Energy. Most are standard Bluetooth SIG or vendor services — any BLE tool recognizes them. The File Transfer Service has its own reference: BLE-File-Transfer-Service.md.

Security. The watch requires a bonded, encrypted connection. Pair with the watch before reading or writing any characteristic.

Services

The standard Bluetooth SIG and Nordic services follow their published specifications — refer to those documents for field formats. The File Transfer Service is UNA’s (Adafruit-based); see its own reference.

Nordic UART (6E400001-B5A3-F393-E0A9-E50E24DCCA9E)

A Nordic UART Service (serial-style channel). Note the two data characteristics are swapped relative to Nordic’s usual convention:

Bluetooth SIG service/characteristic formats are defined by the Bluetooth SIG; the File Transfer Service is based on the Adafruit protocol (see its reference).
